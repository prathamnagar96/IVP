# Phase 2B field protocol

1. Confirm permits, weather envelope, exclusion zones, communications, and
   emergency plan before travelling.
2. Place independent check points on stable ground. Targets on moving ground
   are tracking/check markers and never control points.
3. Record base-station coordinates and antenna height; verify RTK/PPK fix.
4. Complete the flight and inspect coverage, point density, image sharpness,
   and GNSS status on site. Re-fly gaps before leaving when safe.
5. Upload raw data immutably with a manifest and checksums. Do not release a
   product until QC returns `PASS` or an expert reviews `WARN`.
